Also on the water: Tiny Marine Traffic

Tiny Marine Traffic is a sibling to Tiny ATC for ships. A small window onto your favorite waterway, right in the Windows notification area, with real vessel reports from Open Waters AIS.

  • Native harbor map with ship silhouettes that follow reported heading or course, moving ships only by default
  • Select a ship for name, MMSI, type, speed, course, status, destination and report age
  • Save your own areas, from Oslofjord to San Francisco Bay, and set a home. No account or API key

Requires Windows 10 1809 or later, x64 or ARM64. Not for navigation. No release published yet.
